# TRAINING.md — Eight-Week Charm–Persuasion–Advancement Curriculum

Each week has drills (solo), sparring (with a partner), and a scoring rubric. Do the reps. Charm is a skill; skills come from reps.

---

## Week 1 — Reputation audit (Layer 0)

**Drill:** Write down what you believe 5 key people think of you. Then classify each belief as observed / corroborated / inferred / hypothesized / unknown. For every "hypothesized", design one way to verify it this week (ask for feedback directly, check who seeks your help, re-read your last written review).

**Sparring:** Ask a trusted colleague: "What's the one thing you'd say I'm known for here?" Don't defend, just record.

**Rubric:** By end of week you have at least 3 corroborated facts about your reputation and a written uncertainty list.

## Week 2 — Value inventory (Layer 1)

**Drill:** List 10 things you offer. Score each 1–5 on scarcity and 1–5 on visibility. Multiply. Pick the top two.

**Sparring:** Ask a partner to rank your top-3 values from their perspective. Compare with yours; explain any gap.

**Rubric:** Top-2 values chosen with a plan to raise one visibility score by one point.

## Week 3 — Charm fundamentals (Layer 3)

**Drill (daily):** In every conversation, do one of: use the person's name naturally once, ask one follow-up about something they mentioned last time, or put your phone face-down and away. Log each evening.

**Sparring:** Retell a 90-second story (a win, a lesson, something funny) to a partner. Partner scores presence, warmth, expressiveness, attentiveness each 1–5.

**Rubric:** 5 logged days and a partner story score averaging 3.5+.

## Week 4 — The statement (Protocol 1)

**Drill:** Write 5 opening statements using the template `[fact] + [why it matters to them] + [what we'll decide]`. Fact-check each one. Any statement you can't verify is scrapped.

**Sparring:** Deliver one statement to a partner cold. Partner answers only: "Do I know why this matters to me?" If no, revise.

**Rubric:** 3 statements pass a truth check and a partner value check.

## Week 5 — Two questions and a confirmation (Protocol 2)

**Drill:** For each of 3 real upcoming conversations, write Question 1 (position), Question 2 (priority), and the confirmation sentence.

**Sparring:** Partner plays your real counterpart. You may ask only two questions before proposing the confirmation. Partner responds to the confirmation with yes / no / "that's not what I said" — the third outcome trains listening.

**Rubric:** In 3 rounds, you reached an explicit "agreed" twice without asking a third question.

## Week 6 — The clock (Protocol 3)

**Drill:** List every deadline in your current work context that is REAL (review cycles, launches, budget windows). For one goal, attach the real clock to a confirmed priority and draft the sentence. Also write your personal stop-date.

**Sparring:** Partner tries to goad you into inventing urgency ("just tell them it's urgent"). You must produce a clock with a source or decline to use a clock. Invented clocks are an automatic fail.

**Rubric:** One real-clock sentence drafted with source documented, plus a written stop-date.

## Week 7 — The full protocol stack (Layers 4–8)

**Drill:** Run one real persuasive interaction end-to-end: statement → two questions → confirmation → clocked ask → follow-through record.

**Sparring:** Full role-play with a partner using the scoring sheet below.

**Scoring sheet (each 1–5):**
1. Statement: true, audience-framed, no ask inside
2. Q1 reveals actual position
3. Q2 reveals actual priority
4. Confirmation is checkable and gets an explicit yes
5. Clock is real and attached to their priority
6. Exit honored (stop-date respected; a "no" accepted gracefully)

**Rubric:** Total 24/30 or better, with no score of 1 in items 1, 4, or 5 (truth, confirmation, clock are pass/fail floors).

## Week 8 — Mirror test and career plan (Layers 9–11)

**Drill:** Take your Week 7 interaction and run the four mirror-test questions on your actual messages. Write your sponsor-development list: who sees your work, who repeats your name, who has power + incentive.

**Sparring:** Partner red-teams one of your planned asks as if they were the recipient reading it aloud to their team.

**Rubric:** Mirror test passed with evidence; sponsor list has 3 names with one concrete action each.

---

## Standing rules for trainees

1. Never invent a fact or a deadline. Automatic expulsion from the exercise.
2. Three exchanges, then stop — your time-box is binding on you.
3. A defensible "no" is a successful outcome; a pressured "yes" is a failed one.
4. Every agreement gets a written record within 24 hours.
5. Reciprocity is banked silently. Score-keeping out loud cancels the deposit.
