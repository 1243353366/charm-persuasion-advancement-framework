The Charm–Persuasion–Advancement Framework

Training edition · Layers 0–11 · Three core protocols · Mirror test · Decision gate. Runs entirely in your browser.

Core protocols

1. Start with a statement. Open with a verified fact + why it matters to them + what the conversation decides. No ask inside the statement.

2. Two questions and a confirmation. Q1: their position. Q2: their priority. Then restate it as a checkable commitment and get an explicit yes. Never ask for anything before the confirmation lands.

3. Time-based limitation. Attach only real deadlines (documented source) to their confirmed priority. Time-box your own push: three exchanges, then stop and honor it.

Framework layers

Mirror test & decision gate

Mirror test — all four must pass before any ask goes out:

  • Would I be comfortable if this message were read aloud by the recipient to their team?
  • Does the statement survive fact-checking?
  • Is the deadline real?
  • Would I still want this relationship after the interaction?

Decision gate: layers 0–2 mapped · statement verified · questions and confirmation written · clock real and stop-date set · mirror test passed · you can accept a no without relationship damage.

Stopping conditions: clock expired, priority unservable, success would require an invented fact or fake deadline, compromised consent, or cost exceeding value. Escalating pressure is never the next step — re-map, redesign, or exit.
